Aftercare

Keep your lashes looking beautiful for longer

Retention is about half technique and half aftercare. Follow these and you’ll get noticeably more life out of every set.

Keep them dry for 24 hours

Avoid water, steam, saunas and heavy sweating for the first 24 hours while the adhesive fully cures.

Cleanse gently, but do cleanse

Use a lash-safe foaming cleanser and a soft brush every day. Dirty lashes shed faster — clean lashes last.

Avoid oil

Oil breaks down adhesive. Check your cleanser, moisturiser, sunscreen and makeup remover for oils near the eye.

No lash curlers, no waterproof mascara

Extensions already have the perfect curl. Curlers snap them, and waterproof mascara is almost impossible to remove safely.

Be mindful sleeping

Try to sleep on your back, or use a silk pillowcase to reduce friction. Side-sleepers usually see one side thin out first.

Book regular fills

Every two to three weeks keeps a set looking full and costs less than starting again from scratch.

Brows

After a lamination

Dry for 24 hours

No water, steam or sweat on the brows while the new shape sets.

Brush them daily

Brush upward and outward each morning to keep the shape sitting the way it was set.

Nourish the hair

A little brow oil or serum at night keeps laminated hairs conditioned and prevents brittleness.

Space your treatments

Leave at least six weeks between laminations so the hair stays healthy.

Something not sitting right?

Message within 48 hours of your appointment and it will be sorted. Concerns are always easier to fix early.
