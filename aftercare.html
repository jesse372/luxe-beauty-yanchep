Retention is about half technique and half aftercare. Follow these and you’ll get noticeably more life out of every set.

Lash extensions

The six rules

Keep them dry for 24 hours

Avoid water, steam, saunas and heavy sweating while the adhesive fully cures.

Cleanse gently, but do cleanse

A lash-safe foaming cleanser and a soft brush daily. Dirty lashes shed faster — clean lashes last.

Avoid oil

Oil breaks down adhesive. Check your cleanser, moisturiser, sunscreen and makeup remover.

No lash curlers or waterproof mascara

Extensions already have the perfect curl, and waterproof mascara is near impossible to remove safely.

Be mindful sleeping

Sleep on your back where you can, or use a silk pillowcase. Side-sleepers see one side thin out first.

Book regular fills

Every two to three weeks keeps a set looking full — and costs less than starting again.

The daily routine

How to wash lash extensions

Washing your lashes is the part people skip because it feels risky. It is the opposite. Skin oil, makeup and sunscreen collect at the base of the extensions and break down the adhesive all day, so a clean set is the one that lasts.

Once a day, wet your lashes with cool water and work a lash-safe foaming cleanser through them with a soft brush, moving downwards from the lid to the tips. Rinse, pat dry with a lint-free towel rather than rubbing, and brush through with a clean spoolie once they are dry.

Cotton pads and face cloths catch on extensions and pull them. Fingertips or a soft brush only.

Check your bathroom shelf

What breaks a set down

01

Oil

Cleansing oils and balms, micellar water with oil, rich eye creams and most sunscreens. If it goes near your eyes, check the label.

02

Friction

Rubbing your eyes, towelling your face, sleeping face down, and picking at a lash that feels loose. Brush it instead.

03

Heat and steam

Fine in small doses after the first day. Regular saunas, steam rooms and very hot showers soften the adhesive and shorten the life of a set.

Brows & tinting

After a tint

Dry for 24 hours

No water, steam or sweat on the area while the tint settles.

Go easy on cleansers

Oil-based cleansers and exfoliants fade a tint faster. Cleanse around it gently.

Expect it to soften

A tint fades gradually over a few weeks. Rebook when it has lost its depth.

Questions

Aftercare, answered

How soon can I get lash extensions wet?

After 24 hours. The adhesive needs a full day to cure, so keep water, steam and heavy sweat away from your lashes until then.

How do I wash lash extensions?

Once a day with a lash-safe foaming cleanser. Work it gently through the lashes with a soft brush, rinse with cool water, pat dry and brush through with a clean spoolie.

Can I wear mascara or eyeliner with lash extensions?

You should not need mascara, and waterproof or oil-based mascara will break down the adhesive. If you wear eyeliner, choose an oil-free one and remove it with an oil-free remover.

What should I avoid with lash extensions?

Oil-based products around the eyes, rubbing or picking, lash curlers, waterproof mascara, and water or steam in the first 24 hours.

Can I swim with lash extensions?

Yes, after the first 24 hours. Rinse with fresh water afterwards, pat dry and cleanse that evening.

How often do I need a refill?

Every two to three weeks. Beyond three weeks there is usually too little of the set left to refill and a new full set is needed.

How do I look after a brow or lash tint?

Keep the area dry for 24 hours, then go easy on oil-based cleansers and exfoliants, which fade a tint faster. A tint softens gradually over a few weeks.
