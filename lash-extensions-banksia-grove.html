Questions from Banksia Grove

Good to know

How far is Banksia Grove from the Yanchep studio?

About 22 kilometres, roughly twenty-three minutes via Wanneroo Road and Flynn Drive.

Is it worth the drive from an inland suburb?

It depends on what you book. For a single brow tint, probably not. For a full set, a lift and lamination combination, or a standing refill with a technician you trust, Banksia Grove clients generally say yes — and tend to book combinations to make one trip do the work of two.

Can I combine lashes and brows to save a trip?

Yes, and it is recommended for anyone coming from inland. A lash lift and tint with brow lamination and tint is $160 together rather than $175 separately, and runs about two hours in one sitting.
