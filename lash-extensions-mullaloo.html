Why Mullaloo clients come here

Worth the 30 minutes

More Mullaloo enquiries end in a lash lift than in extensions, and that is the right outcome rather than a lost sale. If you are in the water most days, a lift gives you six to eight weeks with nothing to maintain and nothing to lose, where a set of extensions would be asking for a fortnightly refill and a lot of driving.

Mullaloo Beach, the surf club and Tom Simpson Park are the local landmarks; Marmion Avenue runs the whole way north.

Questions from Mullaloo

Good to know

How far is the studio from Mullaloo?

About 28 kilometres, or thirty minutes north on Marmion Avenue. It is towards the outer edge of the regular catchment.

Is it worth a thirty minute drive for a lash lift?

A lift lasts six to eight weeks with no refills, so it is one trip per cycle rather than a fortnightly commitment. For swimmers coming from Mullaloo that maths usually works; a set of extensions needing refills every two weeks generally does not.

Can I swim after a lash lift?

Keep them dry for the first 24 hours while the lift sets. After that there are no restrictions at all — swim, surf, shower, no special products required.
