Why Mullaloo clients come here

Worth the 30 minutes

Mullaloo clients are in the water more than most, and the advice reflects that. A lighter classic or hybrid set on a two-week refill cycle copes with salt water far better than heavy volume, and if your routine makes extensions a poor fit you will be told so plainly rather than sold a set that will not last.

Mullaloo Beach, the surf club and Tom Simpson Park are the local landmarks; Marmion Avenue runs the whole way north.

Questions from Mullaloo

Good to know

How far is the studio from Mullaloo?

About 28 kilometres, or thirty minutes north on Marmion Avenue. It is towards the outer edge of the regular catchment.

Is it worth a thirty minute drive for lashes?

For the full set, usually yes — it is one long appointment. Refills are the real commitment: every two to three weeks, about an hour each, so be honest with yourself about the drive before you start.

Can I swim with lash extensions?

Yes, once the adhesive has cured — keep them dry for the first 24 hours. After that, rinse with fresh water after the ocean and brush them through once dry. Daily swimmers usually move to a two-week refill cycle.
