Questions

Good to know

How long does a lash lift last?

Six to eight weeks, which is roughly the natural growth cycle of your lashes. There is no maintenance in between.

Lash lift or extensions - which should I get?

A lift suits you if you have decent natural lash length and want something you never have to think about. Extensions add length and density that a lift cannot. If you are unsure, send a photo of your natural lashes and you will get an honest recommendation.

How much is a lash lift in Yanchep?

A lash lift is $75, or $90 with a tint included.

Can I wear mascara after a lash lift?

Yes, after the first 24 hours - though most clients find they stop bothering with it.

Is there any downtime?

Keep them dry for 24 hours while the lift sets. After that there are no restrictions.
