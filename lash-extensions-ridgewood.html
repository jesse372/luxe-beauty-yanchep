Questions from Ridgewood

Good to know

How far is the studio from Ridgewood?

About 15 kilometres, or seventeen minutes via Connolly Drive and Marmion Avenue.

What is brow lamination and how long does it last?

It is a treatment that relaxes and resets the direction your brow hairs grow, so they sit fuller and brushed-up. It lasts six to eight weeks. Lamination is $70, or $85 with a tint, or $95 with tint and shape.

Do I need a patch test first?

For anything involving tint or lash adhesive, a patch test is recommended before your first appointment — ideally 48 hours ahead. For Ridgewood clients it is usually worth combining the patch test with another errand rather than making a separate trip.
