About

A quieter kind of beauty appointment

The studio

Luxe Beauty is a private home studio in Yanchep — no busy salon floor, no overlapping appointments, no being rushed out the door. Just one client at a time, in a calm room, with good lighting and better coffee.

The approach

Every set starts with a proper consult: your eye shape, your natural lash health, your routine and how bold you actually want to go. The goal is enhancement — you should still look like you.

Hygiene

Single-use tools where it matters, full sanitising between every client, and professional-grade products throughout. Lash health comes before a fuller-looking set, every time.

The area

Proudly local to Yanchep and serving Two Rocks, Alkimos, Eglinton and the surrounding northern suburbs — so a great set doesn’t mean a drive down the freeway.
