The studio

One client

Luxe Beauty is a small, private beauty salon in Yanchep that does two things: lashes and brows. It is a home studio — no busy salon floor, no overlapping appointments, no being rushed out the door. Just a calm room, good lighting and the time to do the work properly.

Proudly local, and serving Two Rocks, Alkimos, Eglinton and the surrounding northern suburbs — so a great set doesn’t mean a drive down the freeway.
