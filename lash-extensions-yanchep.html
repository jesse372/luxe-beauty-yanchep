Questions

Good to know

How long do eyelash extensions last?

With a fill every two to three weeks a set stays looking full indefinitely. Your natural lashes shed on their own cycle, so extensions come away with them gradually rather than all at once.

Do lash extensions damage your natural lashes?

Not when they are applied correctly. Weight and length are matched to each natural lash, which is why the consultation matters. If a lighter set is better for your lashes, that is what you will be told.

How much are lash extensions in Yanchep?

A classic full set is $120, hybrid $140, volume $160 and mega volume $180. Refills start at $60 for a two week fill.

Do I need a patch test?

Yes, for your first appointment. It takes two minutes and needs to be done at least 24 hours beforehand.

How should I prepare?

Arrive with clean lashes and no eye makeup, and avoid oily skincare around the eyes. It makes a real difference to how well the set holds.
