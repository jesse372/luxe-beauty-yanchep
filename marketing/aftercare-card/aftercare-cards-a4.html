Lash aftercare

Make them last

01Dry for 24 hoursNo water, steam or heavy sweat while the adhesive cures.
02Cleanse every dayLash-safe foaming cleanser and a soft brush.
03Keep oil awayCheck cleanser, moisturiser, sunscreen, makeup remover.
04No curlers or waterproof mascaraExtensions already hold their curl.
05Sleep on your back if you canOr use a silk pillowcase.
06Infill every 2 to 3 weeksIt costs less than starting again.
