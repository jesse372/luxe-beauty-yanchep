=============================================================
CURRENT STATE (before you start)
=============================================================

The domain currently uses Namecheap FREE EMAIL FORWARDING:
  MX  -> eforward1-5.registrar-servers.com
  SPF -> v=spf1 include:spf.efwd.registrar-servers.com ~all

Forwarding is RECEIVE ONLY. It cannot send. That is why we are
moving to Private Email.

No email address is published on the website yet, so nothing
breaks when the MX records change.


=============================================================
STEP 1 - BUY PRIVATE EMAIL
=============================================================

Namecheap -> Private Email -> choose the Starter plan
(1 mailbox is enough - around $11-14 AUD per year)

Link it to: luxebeautyyanchep.com


=============================================================
STEP 2 - LET NAMECHEAP AUTO-CONFIGURE THE MX RECORDS
=============================================================

Namecheap will offer to set the MX records automatically.
ACCEPT IT. It replaces the forwarding MX with:

  mx1.privateemail.com   priority 10
  mx2.privateemail.com   priority 10

This REPLACES email forwarding. That is expected and correct.


=============================================================
STEP 3 - FIX THE SPF RECORD  (easy to miss, breaks sending)
=============================================================

The SPF record still points at the OLD forwarding service. If it
is not updated, mail sent from bec@ may fail SPF checks and land
in spam.

In Advanced DNS, find the TXT record with this value:

  v=spf1 include:spf.efwd.registrar-servers.com ~all

CHANGE its value to:

  v=spf1 include:spf.privateemail.com ~all

*** DO NOT TOUCH THE OTHER TXT RECORD ***
There is a second TXT record starting with:
  google-site-verification=8o_JfYe62lO8SnI0ap7NtO59ed4rQP8klUbdnXrVRac
That one is the Google Search Console verification. Deleting it
un-verifies the site. Leave it exactly as it is.


=============================================================
STEP 4 - ENABLE DKIM
=============================================================

Private Email dashboard -> the mailbox -> enable DKIM.
Namecheap adds the DNS record for you.

DKIM + correct SPF is what keeps appointment emails out of spam.
Worth doing - it is two clicks.


=============================================================
STEP 5 - CREATE THE MAILBOX
=============================================================

Mailbox: bec@luxebeautyyanchep.com
Set a strong password and save it in your password manager.


=============================================================
STEP 6 - ADD IT TO OUTLOOK
=============================================================

In the Outlook app, add an account, choose IMAP, then enter these
manually (Outlook will not auto-detect a Namecheap mailbox):

INCOMING (IMAP)

  Port:      993
  Security:  SSL/TLS
  Username:  bec@luxebeautyyanchep.com
  Password:  (the mailbox password)

OUTGOING (SMTP)
  Server:    mail.privateemail.com
  Port:      465
  Security:  SSL/TLS
  Username:  bec@luxebeautyyanchep.com
  Password:  (the mailbox password)
  Authentication required: YES

If port 465 is rejected, use port 587 with STARTTLS instead.
