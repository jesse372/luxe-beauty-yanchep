POST 5  (Reel, talking or text-over)  Lash lift vs extensions
"Should I get a lift or extensions?"

A lift curls the lashes you already have. Six to eight weeks, no refills,
nothing to fall out. $75, or $90 with a tint. Good if you swim, train, or
do not want the upkeep.

Extensions add length and density your own lashes cannot. From $120, with
a refill every two to three weeks.

Neither is better. They solve different problems. Send a photo of your
natural lashes and you will get an honest answer, even if it is the
cheaper one.

POST 6  (Static)  Aftercare graphic - you already have one
Four things that decide how long your set lasts:

Dry for the first 24 hours
No oil-based products near the eyes
Brush them through daily with a clean spoolie
Do not pick or rub

In Yanchep the two biggest retention killers are salt water and sunscreen
migrating into the lash line. Rinse with fresh water after the beach and
brush them once dry.
