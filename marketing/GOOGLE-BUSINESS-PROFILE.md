## 8. Photos — READ THIS

**Do not upload the stock photos from the website to Google Business Profile.**

Google's guidelines require photos to genuinely represent the business. Stock imagery can
get a profile flagged, and it is obvious to locals. Worse, a customer who books from a
stock photo and gets a different result is a bad first review.

Upload instead:
- **Logo** — `icons/icon-512.png` in this repo (the LB monogram, genuinely hers)
- **Cover photo** — a real photo of the studio
- **Everything else** — real client work, shot on a phone in good light, posted weekly

Profiles with 100+ photos get meaningfully more calls and direction requests than
profiles with a handful. Volume of *real* photos matters.

---

## 9. Reviews — the actual ranking lever

After proximity and category relevance, **review count and recency** is the biggest
factor in local pack ranking. A brand-new profile with 10 genuine reviews will outrank a
five-year-old profile with 2.

The workflow that works:
1. Get the short review link from the profile dashboard ("Ask for reviews")
2. Ask **at the appointment**, while she is still there and happy — not by text later
3. Send the link the same evening
4. Reply to every single review, including bad ones. Google weights owner responses.

Never buy reviews or post them yourself. Google detects review-velocity anomalies and
suspensions are hard to reverse.

Target: 10 reviews in the first month, then a steady few per week.
