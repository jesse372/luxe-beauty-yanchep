===============================================
  SETTING UP YOUR NEW EMAIL ON OUTLOOK
  bec@luxebeautyyanchep.com
===============================================

This takes about 5 minutes.

You'll need your email password - the one that was set up
when the mailbox was created. Have it ready before you start.


-----------------------------------------------
  THE ONLY 4 THINGS YOU'LL NEED TO TYPE
-----------------------------------------------

Keep this bit handy - you'll be asked for these:

  Email address:  bec@luxebeautyyanchep.com

  Password:       (your mailbox password)

  Server:         mail.privateemail.com
                  (this is the answer to BOTH server
                   questions - incoming and outgoing)

  Username:       bec@luxebeautyyanchep.com
                  (the WHOLE email address, not just "bec")

That last one catches most people out. If it asks for a
username, always type the full email address.


-----------------------------------------------
  STEP BY STEP  (Outlook app on your phone)
-----------------------------------------------

1. Open the Outlook app.

2. Tap your picture or the menu icon in the top left,
   then tap the gear/settings icon at the bottom.

3. Tap "Add Mail Account", then "Add Email Account".

4. Type:  bec@luxebeautyyanchep.com
   Tap Continue.

5. Outlook will try to work it out itself and won't manage
   it. That's normal and expected - nothing has gone wrong.

   When it asks you to choose an account type, tap "IMAP".

6. Type your password in the password box.

7. Look for "Advanced Settings" and turn it ON.
   You should now see boxes for server names.

8. Fill them in:

   INCOMING MAIL
     Server / Hostname:  mail.privateemail.com
     Port:               993
     Security type:      SSL/TLS
     Username:           bec@luxebeautyyanchep.com
     Password:           your password

   OUTGOING MAIL
     Server / Hostname:  mail.privateemail.com
     Port:               465
     Security type:      SSL/TLS
     Username:           bec@luxebeautyyanchep.com
     Password:           your password

9. Tap the tick / Sign In / Done.

That's it. Your email should start loading.


-----------------------------------------------
  IF IT WON'T CONNECT
-----------------------------------------------

Try ONE change first:

  Change the OUTGOING port from 465 to 587
  and set security to STARTTLS instead of SSL/TLS

That fixes it almost every time.

Still stuck? Check these three things:

  - Username is the FULL email address
    (bec@luxebeautyyanchep.com, not just "bec")

  - Server is spelled exactly: mail.privateemail.com
    (no "www", no ".com.au")

  - Password is correct - try logging in at
    privateemail.com in your browser to check it


-----------------------------------------------
  IF YOU'D RATHER NOT BOTHER WITH THE APP
-----------------------------------------------

You don't have to use Outlook at all.

Go to:  privateemail.com

Log in with your email address and password and you have
your full inbox in the browser - nothing to set up, works
on your phone and your laptop, and you can send from
bec@luxebeautyyanchep.com straight away.

Add it to your phone's home screen and it behaves almost
exactly like an app.

The Outlook setup is only worth doing if you prefer having
all your email in one place.


-----------------------------------------------
  TEST IT WHEN YOU'RE DONE
-----------------------------------------------

Send an email from bec@luxebeautyyanchep.com to your
personal address, and check it arrives and shows as coming
FROM bec@luxebeautyyanchep.com.

That's the bit that matters - clients should always see
your business address, never a personal one.
