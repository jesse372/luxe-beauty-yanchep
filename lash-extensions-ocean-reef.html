Why Ocean Reef clients come here

Worth the 27 minutes

Salt, wind and sun are the three things that shorten the life of a set, and Ocean Reef clients get all three. That does not rule extensions out, but it does mean a lighter, better-isolated set with a retention-focused adhesive will outperform a heavy one, and that a two-week refill cycle beats a three-week one.

The boat harbour and marina development are the local anchors, with Marmion Avenue the direct run north.

Questions from Ocean Reef

Good to know

How far is Ocean Reef from the studio?

About 25 kilometres, roughly twenty-seven minutes north on Marmion Avenue.

Do extensions survive being on a boat every weekend?

They do, with a shorter refill cycle. Salt spray and wind loosen adhesive faster than ordinary wear, so expect two-week refills rather than three. Rinse with fresh water after a day out and brush them through once dry.

Which set copes best with salt and wind?

A classic or hybrid set. Lighter extensions put less load on each bond than dense volume fans, so they hold up better between refills when you are on the water every weekend.
