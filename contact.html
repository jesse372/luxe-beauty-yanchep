Good to know

Before you book

Do I need a patch test?

Yes, if it’s your first time having lash extensions, a lash lift, a tint or brow lamination. It takes two minutes and needs to be done at least 24 hours beforehand.

How long will my appointment take?

A full set takes 1.5 to 2.5 hours depending on style. Refills are around an hour. Lash lifts and brow treatments are 45 minutes to an hour.

How should I arrive?

With clean lashes and no eye makeup — no mascara, no oily skincare around the eyes. It makes a real difference to how well your set holds.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. A deposit secures your appointment and comes off your total on the day.

What if I need to reschedule?

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice. Deposits for cancellations inside 24 hours, or no-shows, aren’t transferable.

Can I bring my kids?

It’s a home studio with sharp tools and adhesive fumes, so unfortunately not — and you deserve the two hours to yourself anyway.

How often do I need a fill?

Every two to three weeks. Past three weeks you’ll usually need a full set, so it’s worth staying on schedule.
