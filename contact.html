Good to know

Before you book

Do I need a patch test?

Yes, if it’s your first time having lash extensions, a lash lift, a tint or brow lamination. A patch test takes two minutes and needs to be done at least 24 hours before your appointment.

How long will my appointment take?

A full set of lashes takes between 1.5 and 2.5 hours depending on the style. Refills are around an hour. Lash lifts and brow treatments are 45 minutes to an hour.

How should I arrive?

With clean lashes and no eye makeup, please — no mascara, no oily skincare around the eyes. It makes a real difference to how well your set holds.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. A deposit secures your appointment and comes off your total on the day.

What if I need to reschedule?

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice so the spot can be offered to someone else. Deposits for cancellations inside 24 hours, or no-shows, aren’t transferable.

Can I bring my kids?

It’s a home studio with sharp tools and adhesive fumes, so unfortunately not — and you deserve the two hours to yourself anyway.

How often do I need a fill?

Every two to three weeks. Past three weeks you’ll usually need a full set, which costs more — so it’s worth staying on schedule.
