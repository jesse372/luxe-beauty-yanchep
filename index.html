Questions

Good to know
before you book

Where is Luxe Beauty Yanchep?

Luxe Beauty is a private home studio in Yanchep, WA 6035. Because it is a home studio the street address is not published. It is sent to you with your booking confirmation, and there is free parking.

How much are lash extensions in Yanchep?

A classic full set is $120, hybrid $140, volume $160 and mega volume $180. Every price is published and is the price you pay on the day.

How much is a lash refill?

Classic refills are $60 within two weeks and $75 within three. Hybrid, volume and mega volume refills are priced in proportion to the full set, from $70 to $115. Refilling another technician's work costs a little more.

Do you do eyebrows?

Yes. A brow shape (wax and tweeze) is $25 and a brow shape and tint is $40 when added to a lash appointment. A brow shape and tint with a lash tint is $60 and can be booked on its own.

How do I book?

Online, in about a minute. A $50 deposit confirms your appointment and comes off the total. Online booking closes 24 hours ahead, so for anything sooner call or message the studio.

Do I need a patch test?

Yes, if it is your first time having lash extensions or a tint. It takes two minutes, it is free, and it needs to be done at least 24 hours before your appointment.

How long does a full set of lash extensions take?

About two hours for a classic set and up to two and a half for volume or mega volume. Refills are around an hour. Only one client is booked at a time.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Saturday, 9am to 5pm. Sunday and evening appointments are available by arrangement.

Which areas do you serve?

Yanchep, Two Rocks, Eglinton, Alkimos, Jindalee and Butler make up most of the studio's clients, and people travel from across Perth's northern corridor. Prices are the same wherever you come from.
