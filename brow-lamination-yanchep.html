Questions

Good to know

How long does brow lamination last?

Four to six weeks. Brush them up each morning and use a little brow oil at night to keep the hairs conditioned.

How much is brow lamination in Yanchep?

Lamination is $70, $85 with a tint, or $95 for lamination, tint and shape together.

What is the difference between henna and a tint?

A tint colours the hair only. Henna also stains the skin underneath, which makes sparse areas look fuller and lasts longer on the skin.

Will lamination damage my brow hair?

Not if treatments are spaced at least six weeks apart. It is a chemical process, so over-doing it is the main risk - which is why appointments are spaced deliberately.

Do I need a patch test for brow lamination?

Yes, at least 24 hours before your first appointment.
