How to book

Two ways to have your brows done

With your lashes. A brow shape ($25) or a brow shape and tint ($40) is added to a lash full set or refill and done in the same visit. It adds 20 to 30 minutes, and it is how most clients have their brows done, because one trip covers both.

On their own. The brow shape and tint with lash tint ($60) is a stand-alone appointment of about 45 minutes. Brows shaped, brows tinted and lashes tinted in one sitting. It is the one to choose before an event, or if you do not wear extensions.

Either way you book online, and a $50 deposit confirms the appointment and comes off the total. If you already wear extensions, lash prices are here.

What happens

Your brow appointment

Patch test, if it is your first tint

Two minutes, free, and done at least 24 hours before, preferably 48. Tint is the one part of a brow appointment that can cause a reaction, and this is how it is checked safely.

Mapping

A look at your natural brows, your face shape and how you usually wear them. If one brow sits higher or fuller than the other, which is normal, the plan allows for it.

Shape

Wax and tweeze to the agreed line. If your brows would look better left a little fuller, that is what you will be told, even if you asked for thinner.

Tint

Colour is matched to your hair and skin, applied to the brow, and removed once it has taken. A lash tint, if you are having one, is done in the same sitting.

Aftercare

Keep the area dry for 24 hours and go easy on oil-based cleansers and exfoliants afterwards. Full aftercare.

Timing

How long it lasts

A shape lasts until the hair grows back, which for most people means a tidy-up every four to six weeks. A tint softens gradually over a few weeks. It does not stop suddenly, it just loses depth.

In Yanchep, sun and salt water speed that up a little. Oil-based sunscreen and cleansers do the same, so cleanse around tinted brows gently and they will hold their colour longer.

If you wear lash extensions you are in every two to three weeks for a refill anyway. Adding brows to every second refill keeps both on one schedule, and saves a separate trip.

Questions

Good to know

How much is an eyebrow shape and tint in Yanchep?

At Luxe Beauty Yanchep a brow shape is $25 and a brow shape and tint is $40, added to a lash full set or refill. A brow shape and tint with a lash tint is $60 and can be booked on its own.

Can I book eyebrows without lash extensions?

Yes. The brow shape and tint with lash tint package ($60) is booked as its own appointment. The $25 brow shape and the $40 brow shape and tint are add-ons to a lash full set or refill.

Is the brow shape done by waxing or threading?

Waxing and tweezing. Wax takes care of the fine hair above and below the brow, and tweezing finishes the line and deals with anything the wax should not go near.

How long does a brow tint last?

A tint softens gradually over a few weeks. How long it holds depends on your hair, your skincare and how much sun and salt water your brows see. Oil-based cleansers and exfoliants fade it faster.

Do I need a patch test for a brow tint?

Yes, if you have not had a tint at the studio before. It takes two minutes, it is free, and it needs to be done at least 24 hours before your appointment, preferably 48.

How long does a brow appointment take?

The brow shape and tint with lash tint package takes about 45 minutes. Added to a lash appointment, brows take 20 to 30 minutes on top.

How often should I have my brows done?

Most people are ready for a tidy-up every four to six weeks. If you wear lash extensions, adding brows to every second refill keeps both on the same schedule.

Do you offer brow lamination, henna brows or microblading?

No. The brow menu is deliberately short: shaping by wax and tweeze, and tinting. If one of those other treatments would suit you better, you will be told so.

Should I let my brows grow out before the appointment?

If you can, leave them alone for two to three weeks beforehand. More hair to work with means more choice about the shape.
